Terms & Conditions

Campus Black Box is provided as an educational project and campus infrastructure management facility. By accessing this platform, students, faculty, and administrative users agree to report factual maintenance observations in good faith. Frivolous or malicious submissions are subject to college disciplinary policies.

1. Service Availability

The platform is provided for campus reporting on an as-available basis. Emergency physical or medical hazards must also be reported directly to campus emergency security.

2. User Conduct

Reporters must provide clear descriptions and genuine photographic evidence corresponding to the indicated room or facility.

Privacy Policy

Campus Black Box values user privacy. Reports are linked to authenticated college email accounts to prevent automated spam and enable dispatch confirmation. Personal identities are strictly shielded from public view.

Data Retention & Security

Incident data, resolution timestamps, and uploaded media are retained for facility auditing and continuous infrastructure improvement.

Acceptable Use Policy

Users must utilize Campus Black Box exclusively for legitimate infrastructure, sanitation, electrical, network, and safety reporting on campus premises.

  • Do not submit non-campus or irrelevant personal grievances.
  • Do not upload inappropriate, offensive, or non-facility images.
  • Do not attempt to tamper with QR token deep links or API boundaries.

How to Report an Issue (Help)

  1. Locate the Room QR Code: Scan the physical plaque mounted near room entrances to automatically bind the exact location.
  2. Describe the Problem: Provide concrete details. The integrated AI will suggest the most accurate maintenance category.
  3. Attach a Photo: Take a photo showing the fault clearly (supports JPEG, PNG, WebP up to 5MB).
  4. Track Live Progress: View real-time status updates under the "My Reports" tab.
